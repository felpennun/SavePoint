- **check-secrets.ps1 required the Docker stack.** Task 1's `<verify>` is `check-secrets.ps1`, whose Phases 2–4 scan the running `web` container, both images, and compose logs. The stack was not running at resume; it was brought up (`docker compose -f infra/compose.yaml up --build --wait`) and the gate then passed cleanly. No plan text change; recorded here for calibration.
- **`requests` version knowledge gap.** An earlier note in this session claimed `requests==2.34.2` did not exist (latest 2.32.x). That was a stale-knowledge error — 2.34.2 was published 2026-05-14 and is verified real. The pin is correct.
- **DATA-04 traceability duplication — RESOLVED.** `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` and `ROADMAP.md` previously listed `DATA-04` (and `CAT-02`) under both Phase 01.1 and Phase 4. The author confirmed (2026-09-05) that the Phase 4 entries were leftover bookkeeping from inserting Phase 01.1 and reprioritizing the work forward. The Phase 4 `DATA-04` and `CAT-02` rows were removed; `DATA-04` is now marked complete (ADR-006, this plan); `CAT-02` remains owned by Phase 01.1 (Plan 01.1-03, still pending).
- **Terms interpretation is a reasoned position, not legal advice.** The FAQ-vs-DSA reconciliation in ADR-006 point 4 treats IGDB's published FAQ as the "written authorization … otherwise" the DSA contemplates. This is defensible and recorded verbatim for the thesis (DATA-08), but it is the author's call to accept.
